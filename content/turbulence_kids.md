# Why is it bumpy?

The air is not still. It moves in rivers and bubbles and waves. When the plane flies from fast air into slow air, or from rising air into sinking air, it gets bumped. Like a boat on waves.

## Near the ground

When you take off or land, the sun has warmed the ground and bubbles of warm air are floating up. The plane bounces over them. Higher up, it gets smooth.

## Clouds

Puffy clouds are bubbles of rising air. Flying past them is like driving over speed bumps. Pilots fly around the really big storm clouds.

## Rivers of wind

Very high up there are rivers of wind called jet streams. At the edges of the river the air swirls, and that makes bumps even when the sky is clear and blue.

## Mountain waves

When wind blows over mountains, the air goes up and down in big slow waves, like water going over a rock in a stream. Flying over the Rockies in a strong wind can feel like a very slow roller coaster.

## Is it safe?

Yes. Planes are built to be much, much stronger than any bump. The seatbelt sign is there so nobody bounces out of their seat. Buckle up and enjoy the roller coaster.
