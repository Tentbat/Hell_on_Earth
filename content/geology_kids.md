# Reading the rocks

## Volcanoes

A lonely pointy mountain with snow on top is a volcano. A black patch on the ground with no plants is old lava that came out of one. Small hills shaped like cones with a dent in the top are baby volcanoes.

## Cracks in the Earth

A mountain range with one very straight, very steep side is sitting on a crack in the Earth called a fault. One side went up, the other went down.

## What glaciers left behind

A long skinny lake in a valley was dug out by a glacier, a river of ice. Thousands of little round lakes scattered everywhere are holes left when lumps of ice melted.

## Rivers

A river that splits into lots of strands like a rope is carrying tons of sand and gravel from the mountains. A river that wiggles in big loops is crossing a flat plain.

## Deserts

Sand dunes shaped like crescent moons mean the wind always blows the same way. A flat white patch in the desert is an old lake that dried up and left its salt behind.

## Craters

A round bowl with a raised edge in the desert might be where a rock from space hit the Earth. Meteor Crater in Arizona is nearly a mile wide.

## Colours

- Red rocks have iron in them, like rust.
- White is salt or snow.
- Black is lava.
- Green is plants, and plants mean water.
