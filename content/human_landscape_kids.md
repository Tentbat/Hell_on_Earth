# Things people made

## Crop circles

The green circles are giant round fields. A sprinkler as long as four football fields spins around in the middle, watering the crops. A half circle means a road got in the way.

## Farms

Squares of different colours are fields. Green is a growing crop, gold is ready to harvest, brown is ploughed. Long stripes are wheat farms in dry places.

## Mines

A big bowl with steps down the sides is a mine. People dig down in rings, like a stadium. The strange blue or orange pond next to it is where they put the muddy leftovers.

## Making electricity

- Rows of white toothpicks on hills are wind turbines.
- Dark flashing rectangles are solar panels.
- A huge chimney puffing white cloud by a lake is a power station. The cloud is steam, not smoke.
- A straight wall across a river is a dam, with a long lake behind it.

## Ships

Ships leave a white line behind them in the water. A long line means a fast ship. At the port, giant cranes lift boxes on and off.

## Cities

Tall buildings cast long shadows in the middle. Houses with curly streets are around the edges. The longest straight lines are the airport runways.
