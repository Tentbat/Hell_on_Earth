# Lightning from above

## Thunderstorm towers

A thunderstorm looks like a giant tower of cloud with a flat top, like an anvil or a mushroom. Some are taller than the plane is high!

## You cannot hear it

Lightning is loud on the ground, but in the plane you only see the flashes. At night a storm far away flickers like a lamp inside the cloud.

## Pilots go around

Pilots never fly through thunderstorms. They have a radar in the nose of the plane that shows where the rain is, and they steer around it. Sometimes you feel little bumps from the edges.

## Red jellyfish in the sky

Way above big storms there are flashes called sprites. They look like red jellyfish and last less than a blink. Hardly anyone has seen one. Maybe you will.
