# Clouds from the top

You are seeing clouds from above. Almost nobody gets to do that!

## Puffy cotton balls (cumulus)

Little white puffs with flat bottoms. Each one is sitting on a bubble of warm air rising from the ground. They love sunny afternoons.

## The lumpy blanket (stratocumulus)

A big grey and white blanket with lumps and gaps. It is very common over the sea.

## Fog in the valleys (stratus)

A smooth white sheet with hills poking out like islands. People down there cannot see the sun. You can.

## Thunderstorm towers (cumulonimbus)

Giant clouds that go up higher than the plane, with a flat top like an anvil. Pilots fly around them. At night you might see lightning flashing inside.

## Wisps up high (cirrus)

Thin streaks made of ice. If they are above you, they are very, very high.

## Cloud streets

Rows and rows of little clouds all in lines, like streets. The wind is rolling the air into tubes.

## UFO clouds (lenticular)

Smooth clouds shaped like a flying saucer, sitting still above mountains. The air is bouncing over the mountain like water over a rock in a stream.
