# Lines on the land

## Borders

Most borders are invisible from the sky. But sometimes you can see them, because people do different things on each side. The line between the United States and Mexico near California is a dead-straight edge: green fields on one side, brown desert on the other.

## Why the middle of America looks like a chessboard

More than 200 years ago, people measured the land into squares one mile wide and built roads along the edges. From the plane the whole country looks like graph paper. Every square is a farm.

## Rivers

A river that wiggles is crossing flat land. A river in a dark slot is in a canyon. The little curved ponds next to a river are old wiggles the river left behind.

## The Continental Divide

This is a line along the top of the Rocky Mountains. Rain that falls on one side flows all the way to the Pacific Ocean. Rain on the other side flows to the Atlantic Ocean. One raindrop, two very different journeys.
