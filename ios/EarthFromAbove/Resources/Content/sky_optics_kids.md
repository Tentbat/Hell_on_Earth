# Sky magic

## Your own rainbow ring

Look down at the clouds on the side of the plane away from the sun. Around the plane's shadow there might be a small ring of rainbow colours. It is called a glory, and it is made just for you: everyone sees their own.

## Sun rings

A big ring around the sun is made by tiny ice crystals high in the sky, bending the light.

## The Earth's shadow

After the sun goes down, look the other way. A dark band climbs the sky. That is the shadow of the whole Earth! Above it is a pink band.

## Plane trails

The white lines behind planes are made of ice. The engine makes water, and the water freezes in the super cold air up here. In dry air the trail is short. In wet air it lasts for hours.

## Northern lights

On a night flight far to the north, you might see green curtains waving in the sky. Turn off your light and look toward the north. Those are the northern lights.

## City lights

At night a city looks like a map made of light. Orange lights are old street lamps, white ones are new. A row of red lights all blinking together is a wind farm.
